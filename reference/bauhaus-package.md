# bauhaus: 'ggplot2' Themes from the Bauhaus Clock Screensaver Dials

Sixteen 'ggplot2' themes, one for each day dial of the Bauhaus Clock
screensaver. Each theme paints the plot on the dial's paper plate,
complete with the screensaver's fine paper grain, sets type in the
screensaver's typeface (Zalando Sans Expanded, bundled), and takes its
text, grid, geom and palette colours from the dial's measured inks,
steel, lume and shadow tints. Matching discrete, continuous and binned
colour scales are included.
